Road to Devcon V — Problem 2 · “Take your records with you”

TAKE YOUR RECORDS WITH YOU

Store your bird sightings as portable records that independent software can read — not data trapped inside one app.

Why this matters

Exporting data is not the same as owning a portable record.

Traditional flow

App → Export → Another app must understand the export

Portable-record flow

Writer → Self-describing record → Swarm → Independent Reader

Each record carries its own format, version, and data — so software that never shipped with this Writer can still understand it.
